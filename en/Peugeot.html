The car

Peugeot 9X8

The two 9X8 cars (#93 and #94) use a 2.6 L twin-turbo V6 and an electric motor on the front axle. Like almost all hypercars, they leave the pit lane on electric power before switching to the combustion engine.

At first Peugeot made a bold bet: no rear wing. This reduced downforce and the car struggled against its rivals. For 2024 Peugeot unveiled a completely revised 9X8 with a rear wing.

The Lion has three Le Mans wins (1992, 1993 and 2009) and is still chasing its first podium with the 9X8.
