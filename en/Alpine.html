The car

Alpine A424

The Alpine A424 is an LMDh built on an Oreca chassis, with a 3.4 L turbo V6 developed with Mecachrome.

Alpine is no stranger to Le Mans: in 1978 the Renault-Alpine A442B won the 24 Hours. The brand then won many times in the lower classes before stepping up to Hypercar.

Since 2024, A424s #35 and #36 have been part of the top-class field.
