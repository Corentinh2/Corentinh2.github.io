The car

Glickenhaus SCG 007 LMH

The SCG 007 was dreamed up by Jim Glickenhaus, an American enthusiast who built his own hypercar to take on Toyota at Le Mans. It uses a 3.5-litre twin-turbo V8 with no hybrid system.

Its best result was third place in 2022, before the 2023 centenary which was its last appearance. Lacking the budget of the big manufacturers, the team did not return in 2024.
