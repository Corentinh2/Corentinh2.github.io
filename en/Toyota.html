The car

Toyota TR010 Hybrid

Toyota enters two cars in the Hypercar class, #7 and #8. For 2026 the GR010 gave way to the TR010 Hybrid, a thorough evolution with new bodywork and a red, white and black livery. The team is now called Toyota Racing.

Under the carbon bodywork sits a 3.5 L twin-turbo V6, a 7-speed sequential gearbox and an electric motor on the front axle.

After five wins in a row from 2018 to 2022, Toyota lost three times to Ferrari. In 2026 the team struck back: victory for car #7 (Conway, Kobayashi, de Vries) and third place for #8.

Look out during the race: the lights on the sides show the position in class. One light for the leader, two for second, three for third.
