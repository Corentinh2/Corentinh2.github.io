Visitin 2027

The 95th edition will be raced on 12 and 13 June 2027. Here is the known schedule and our tips to make the most of it.

📅

The week's schedule

Detailed times are published by the organiser in the spring: always check the official website before you travel.

  1. Early June

    Scrutineering in town

    Cars and drivers go through technical checks in the centre of Le Mans. Free and very friendly.

  2. Sun 6 June

    Test day

    The teams' first laps of the circuit.

  3. Wed 9 June

    Practice and qualifying

    The official week opens with the first sessions on track.

  4. Thu 10 June

    Hyperpole

    The fastest cars fight for pole position.

  5. Fri 11 June

    Track day

    The public can walk on the track in the late afternoon: a unique chance to see the pit straight up close.

  6. Sat 12 June

    Start at 4 pm

    Warm-up at midday, grid walk in the early afternoon, then the start. Night show in the evening.

  7. Sun 13 June

    Finish at 4 pm

    The chequered flag, then the podium in front of the pits.

Practical info

🚋

The tram

Line T1 links the station and the city centre to the circuit (Antarès-MMArena stop). It is often faster than driving.

🏕️

Campsites

Several official campsites surround the circuit. The atmosphere is unique, but places go fast.

👀

Where to watch

The grandstands opposite the pits, but also corners accessible with your ticket: Tertre Rouge, Arnage, Mulsanne…

🏛️

The museum

The 24 Hours of Le Mans museum, at the circuit entrance, covers a century of racing with many winning cars.

24Le Mans Hypercars

A fan site about the hypercars of the 24 Hours of Le Mans, from the centenary race to today.
