A to Z

Theglossary

All the words you will hear during the 24 Hours, explained simply.

ABCDFGHLMPSTW
A
ACO
Automobile Club de l'Ouest: the association that has organised the 24 Hours of Le Mans since 1923.
B
Balance of Performance (BoP)
Adjustment of each car's weight and power so that very different models stay on equal terms.
Blue flag
Tells a driver that a faster car is coming and must be let through.
Box
The space reserved for each car in the pit lane, where the mechanics work.
C
Chequered flag
The black and white flag that marks the end of the race.
Chicane
A tight sequence of corners added to slow cars down, as on the Mulsanne Straight.
D
Double stint
When a driver does two stints in a row without handing over.
F
Formation lap
The lap behind the safety car before the start.
Full Course Yellow (FCY)
The whole track is neutralised: speed limited to 80 km/h and no overtaking.
G
Grid walk
The moment before the start when the public can walk on the grid among the cars.
H
Hybrid
A car combining a combustion engine and an electric motor.
Hypercar
The top class, the one fighting for overall victory.
Hyperpole
The final qualifying session that decides pole position.
L
LMDh
Hypercar rules with a standard chassis and hybrid system, shared with the American IMSA series.
LMGT3
The class for GT cars derived from road models, since 2024.
LMH
Le Mans Hypercar: rules under which the manufacturer designs its car freely.
LMP2
The class of identical prototypes (Oreca chassis, Gibson engine) for private teams.
M
Mulsanne Straight
The famous straight of almost 6 km (Les Hunaudières), broken by two chicanes.
P
Pit stop
A stop in the pits to refuel energy, change tyres or change driver.
Pole position
First place on the starting grid, earned by the fastest car in qualifying.
Position lights
Lights on the sides of hypercars: 1, 2 or 3 lights for the top three in class.
S
Safety car
A car that groups the competitors behind it. Le Mans uses three.
Scrutineering
The technical checks of the cars, held in the city centre before the race.
Slow Zone
A section of track limited to 80 km/h during an intervention, a Le Mans speciality.
Stint
The time a driver spends at the wheel, or the distance between two pit stops.
T
Team
The outfit that enters the cars: drivers, engineers, mechanics.
W
WEC
World Endurance Championship, of which Le Mans is the flagship round.
