Pit stopgame

A pit stop is won by tenths of a second. Brake in the right place, change the tyres, refuel and leave at the right moment!

Time0.00 s
Penalty0 s
Best—

  1. 1. Stop at the boxClick (or Space) when the car is on the yellow zone.
  2. 2. TyresClick each flashing wheel as fast as you can.
  3. 3. RefuelHold the button and release in the green zone.
  4. 4. ReleaseWait for the green light before clicking. Too early = penalty!
